Frequently Asked Questions

What is PosterWords?

PosterWords is an iOS app that turns learning Mandarin Chinese into collecting beautifully designed poster cards. Instead of grinding random vocabulary lists, you describe a real-life scene and AI generates a complete learning bundle with words, phrases, and sentences — all with tone-marked pinyin, native audio, and smart review.

How does the AI scene generation work?

Simply describe any scene or situation in English (or your native language). PosterWords' AI instantly generates 5 contextual words, 4 practical phrases, and 3 complete sentences — all tailored to your chosen HSK level with accurate tone-marked pinyin and natural translations.

Is PosterWords free to use?

PosterWords is free to download with basic features. PosterWords Pro subscription unlocks unlimited AI scene generation and full access to all learning features. Your subscription supports ongoing development.

What HSK levels are supported?

PosterWords supports all levels from HSK 1 to HSK 9 under the new HSK 3.0 standard. Whether you're a complete beginner or preparing for advanced proficiency, the AI adapts vocabulary difficulty to your target level.

Can I practice writing Chinese characters?

Yes! PosterWords includes Hanzi stroke writing practice where you trace characters stroke by stroke with guided, animated demonstrations. Build muscle memory alongside your vocabulary knowledge.

Does PosterWords work offline?

Cards you've already generated are available offline for review. AI scene generation requires an internet connection. Your progress syncs via iCloud when you're back online.

What devices does PosterWords support?

PosterWords is available on iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. Your progress syncs across all your Apple devices via iCloud.
